## Contribution Guidelines

1. **Benchmarking Safety**: Ensure CPU and GPU stress tests properly respect termination signals and timer clamps.
2. **Cross-Browser Verification**: Validate stress modules across Chromium-based, Firefox, and WebKit rendering engines.
3. **Collaboration**: Follow standard git commit conventions and attribution for multi-developer contributions.

Contributions, bug reports, and optimizations are welcome! Feel free to open an issue or pull request.
